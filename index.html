Based in Toronto, Canada

Hi, I'm Sanjana 👋

Senior AI Researcher with 9 years of experience in generative AI, NLP and computer vision. I bridge research and production systems, and I've authored four peer-reviewed publications, including a WACV 2024 paper on real-time, edge-deployable image style transfer.

Since 2017 I've built face recognition, license plate recognition, person tracking and re-identification, and Thai-language OCR systems, several now in real-world use. My face recognition model ranked in the top 20 globally on the NIST FRVT leaderboard, and my landmark localisation work was published in IEEE Access.

One of my favorite projects was brain decoding: I led research on reconstructing the images people see from 3D fMRI brain scans, using self-supervised learning (JEPA), diffusion models and world models.

Today I'm a Senior Data Scientist at Valeris in Toronto, building an LLM-powered system that turns long healthcare policy documents into structured data. I've also supervised small teams of data scientists along the way.
